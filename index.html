Field Manual

🚶 Move

WASD / arrows (or the touch joystick) to walk; click or tap the floor to walk there. Walk up to a console and press E / Space (✋ on mobile). Q rotates the view, wheel / pinch zooms, 1–7 fast-travels. Breaches and faults are physical spots in the station: go there.
WASD/方向键或触屏摇杆移动；走到控制台前按 E/空格（手机点 ✋）互动。

📞 Contacts

Mira on Ganymede and Captain Reyes on the inbound Kestrel call live. Mission Control and your family call from Earth only during comm windows, and their replies arrive late. Answer at the Comms console. What you choose matters.
木卫三的米拉和红隼号的雷耶斯可实时通话；地球的控制中心和家人只能在通信窗口联络，且有光延迟。到通讯台接听。

👽 Events

Strange things happen out here: derelicts, moonlets, flares, a stowaway — and a signal that should not exist. Events pause the clock and ask you to choose.

📡 Data → Science

Science only counts once uplinked. Lock the dish onto Earth during a comm window (blue on the timeline). Storage is limited.

⚡ Power & Heat

The reactor powers everything. More output = more heat. Balance coolant loops or the core SCRAMs.

🫧 Life Support

The O₂ scrubber and water recycler wear down. Service them before they fail. Seal hull breaches fast.

☢ Radiation

Storms are announced shortly before they hit. Raise the shield and shelter in the storm cellar (Reactor). Low orbit = 2× dose, 1.65× science.

💛 Morale

Solitude wears you down. Watch Jupiter from the dome, tend plants, write the journal, read mail from home.

On Cozy, the station is patient. On Survival, it is not. Uplink what light you can before the Kestrel arrives. Somewhere in the Europa data there may be more.
治愈模式里，空间站很有耐心。高压生存则不然。在红隼号抵达前，尽量把光传回去。
